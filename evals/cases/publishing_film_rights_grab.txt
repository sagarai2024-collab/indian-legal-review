AUTHOR-PUBLISHER AGREEMENT

This agreement is made at Pune on 2 March 2026 between Tara Bhatt (the "Author") and Riverstone Publications Pvt. Ltd. (the "Publisher").

1. The Author grants the Publisher the sole and exclusive licence for a period of 5 years to print and publish the book "Salt and Saffron" in any material form, to perform the work in public, to make any cinematograph film or sound recording in respect of the work, to make translations in any language and to make adaptations of the work, throughout the world.

2. The Publisher shall pay the Author a royalty of 8% of the MRP of printed copies and 25% of net receipts on e-books.

3. The Publisher shall make up accounts on an annual basis as at 31 March and pay by 30 October.

4. In the event of any claim, the Publisher may retain any sum due to the Author under this or any other agreement between the parties until the claim is settled.

5. The cover design, price and promotion shall be the Publisher's responsibility and the Publisher reserves the right to make the final decision on all such matters.

6. The Publisher may remainder overstock and no royalty shall be payable on copies sold as remainders.

7. The Author shall give the Publisher the first right to publish the Author's next work.

8. All rights shall revert to the Author if the work is out of print for twelve months.

9. Disputes shall be referred to arbitration seated at Pune under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this agreement.
Signed by the Author
Signed for the Publisher

FILM RIGHTS ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 15 March 2025 at Mumbai between Meera Sen (the "Author"), author of the published memoir "Monsoon Letters" (ISBN 978-81-0000-000-0) (the "Book"), and Silverframe Studios LLP (the "Producer").

1. Assignment. In consideration of Rs. 25,00,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Lakh), the Author irrevocably assigns to the Producer the sole and exclusive right to produce cinematograph films, web-series and television series based on the Book, in all media now known or hereafter devised, throughout the world, in perpetuity.

2. Consideration. The sum in Clause 1 is the full and final consideration for all rights granted. The Author waives all rights to any royalty or share of revenue from satellite, OTT, streaming, music or any other exploitation of the film, and shall not be entitled to any further royalty.

3. Work for hire. The screenplay adaptation and any material contributed by the Author shall be a work made for hire and the Producer shall be deemed the first owner of copyright in it.

4. Moral rights. The Author waives all moral rights, including the right to claim authorship and the right to object to any change, distortion or modification of the Book in the film.

5. Derivative rights. The Producer shall have the right to make sequels, prequels, remakes and spin-offs without any further payment to the Author.

6. Credit. The film shall carry the credit "Based on the book Monsoon Letters by Meera Sen" in the opening titles.

7. Governing law and disputes. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator, seated at Mumbai, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

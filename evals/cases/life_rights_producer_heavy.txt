LIFE RIGHTS AND BOOK ADAPTATION AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on 12 January 2026 between Kaveri Films LLP (the "Producer"), Nandini Ghosh, author of the memoir "Paper Boats" based on her life story (the "Personality"), and Inkwell Literary LLP (the "Agency").

1. Definitions. "Life Story" means the Personality's name, likeness and the incidents of her life as recorded in the book or provided by her, whether preceding, surrounding or following the date of this agreement, till the release of the film.

2. Grant. The Personality irrevocably assigns to the Producer all rights in the Life Story and the book for a feature film, throughout the universe, in perpetuity, including remake, sequel and series rights.

3. Fictionalisation. The Producer may fictionalise, dramatise and add fictional characters and events in its sole discretion. The Producer shall have the final creative control on the film and the script.

4. Moral rights. The Personality irrevocably waives all moral rights.

5. Exclusivity. The Personality shall not participate in any audio-visual production, podcast or documentary relating to her life story, and shall not, in perpetuity, engage with any third party for audio-visual content based on the Life Story.

6. No challenge. The Personality and the Agency shall not seek to challenge, through the courts or otherwise, the rights of the Producer to exploit the film.

7. Remuneration. The Producer shall pay INR 20,00,000, of which INR 18,00,000 is payable to the Personality and INR 2,00,000 is the remuneration to the Agency. The Personality agrees that the remuneration is sufficient and adequate for the rights and the Producer shall not be liable to make any payment beyond it at any time.

8. Credit and promotion. Credit placement shall be determined by the Producer in its sole discretion, and the Producer shall not be responsible for failure of any distributor to accord credit. The Personality shall make herself available for the publicity and promotion of the film at the Producer's expense.

9. Remedies. The Producer may seek injunctive relief. The Personality shall not be entitled to obtain any injunctive relief and her remedies are strictly limited to the right to recover money damages.

10. Assignment. The Producer may assign any and all rights to any person without any notice to the Personality.

11. Confidentiality. The Personality shall not disclose or post anything about the film or the script on any platform. This clause shall survive this agreement in perpetuity.

12. Jurisdiction. The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this agreement.
Signed by the Producer
Signed by the Personality
Signed by the Agency

OPTION AGREEMENT

Dated 1 September 2026, between Farhan Qureshi (the "Author"), author of the novel "Nightjar", and Bluewater Pictures Pvt. Ltd. (the "Producer").

1. Option. In consideration of an option fee of Rs. 2,00,000, the Author grants the Producer the exclusive option to acquire the film and web-series rights in the novel.

2. Purchase price. If the Producer exercises the option, it shall pay the Author a purchase price of Rs. 30,00,000.

3. Vesting. All film and web-series rights in the novel shall vest in the Producer upon execution of this agreement, irrespective of whether the purchase price has been paid.

4. Credit. The film shall credit the Author as the writer of the original novel.

5. Territory. The rights shall be worldwide.

6. Disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Mumbai.

Signed by the Author
Signed for the Producer

LIFE RIGHTS AGREEMENT

This Life Rights Agreement is made on 20 June 2026 between Priya Das (the "Subject"), for herself and on behalf of her daughter aged 9 years, and Northstar Films (the "Producer").

1. Grant. The Subject grants the Producer the exclusive right to portray the life story of the Subject and of her minor daughter in a feature film and web-series, including the events relating to the sexual abuse of the daughter and the proceedings before the Child Welfare Committee.

2. Creative freedom. The Producer may fictionalise, dramatise and create composite characters in its sole and absolute discretion. The Subject shall have no approval or consultation rights.

3. Release. The Subject irrevocably waives all claims, including claims for defamation, invasion of privacy and any other cause of action, and covenants not to sue the Producer in respect of the film.

4. Consideration. The Producer shall pay the Subject a fee of Rs. 5,00,000.

5. Personal material. The Subject shall provide diaries, medical records, court papers and photographs to the Producer.

Signed by the Subject
Signed for the Producer

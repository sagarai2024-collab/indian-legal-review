AGREEMENT FOR LIFE STORY RIGHTS

This agreement dated 5 October 2026 is made between the legal heirs of Late Shri Arun Bose, namely his wife and his son (together the "Heirs"), and Redhill Media Pte. Ltd. (the "Producer").

1. The Heirs grant the Producer the exclusive life rights of Late Shri Arun Bose, a former football coach, for a biopic, together with the right to use his letters and photographs held by the Heirs.

2. The Producer shall pay the Heirs Rs. 10,00,000 and a credit acknowledging the family's cooperation.

3. The Producer shall keep personal data and family documents confidential and return them after production.

4. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of Singapore and the courts of Singapore shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Signed by the Heirs
Signed for the Producer

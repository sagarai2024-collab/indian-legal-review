PUBLISHING AGREEMENT

This Publishing Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 10 February 2026 between Ananya Rao, residing at Bengaluru (the "Author"), and Lotus Leaf Books Pvt. Ltd., having its office at Bengaluru (the "Publisher").

1. The Work. The Author has written a non-fiction book titled "Rivers Remember" (manuscript dated 5 January 2026), described in Schedule A (the "Work").

2. Grant of Rights. The Author assigns to the Publisher the exclusive right to print, publish and sell the Work in book form, in print and e-book, in the English language. All other rights, including translation, audio, film, television and stage rights, are reserved by the Author.

3. Territory. The rights granted in Clause 2 extend worldwide.

4. Period. The rights granted shall be for a period of ten (10) years from the date of this Agreement.

5. Royalty. The Publisher shall pay the Author a royalty of 10% of the printed retail price (MRP) on every print copy sold and 25% of net receipts on every e-book sold, together with a non-refundable advance of Rs. 1,50,000 adjustable against royalties. Statements shall be sent every six months and the Author may audit the Publisher's accounts once a year.

6. Publication. The Publisher shall publish the Work within twelve months of acceptance of the final manuscript.

7. Credit. The Author's name shall appear on the cover, spine and title page of every edition.

8. Reversion. If the Work remains out of print for more than twelve months, or if the Publisher fails to pay any amount due within sixty days of written notice, all rights shall revert to the Author.

9. Warranties. The Author warrants that the Work is original. The Author shall indemnify the Publisher only for breach of this warranty, and the Author's liability shall not exceed the amounts received under this Agreement.

10. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach that is not cured within thirty days of written notice.

11. Disputes. Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru and the language English. Subject to this, the courts at Bengaluru shall have jurisdiction.

12. Stamp duty. This Agreement is executed on non-judicial stamp paper of the value prescribed under the Karnataka Stamp Act.

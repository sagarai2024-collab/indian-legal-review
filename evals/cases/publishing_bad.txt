AGREEMENT FOR PUBLICATION

This agreement is entered into on 3 August 2026 between Ravi Menon (the "Author") and Brightline Press (the "Publisher").

1. The Author hereby assigns to the Publisher all rights in his novel "The Salt Road" and in all future works written by the Author.

2. The Publisher shall pay the Author such amount as the Publisher may decide in its discretion after publication.

3. The Author shall not write for or publish with any other publisher for a period of five years after the termination of this agreement.

4. Any claim by the Author not raised in writing within 30 days of the event shall be barred and the Author's right to claim shall lapse.

5. The Publisher may terminate this agreement at any time without notice.

6. The courts of Kolkata alone shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Signed by the Author
Signed for the Publisher

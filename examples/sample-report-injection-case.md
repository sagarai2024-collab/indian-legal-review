## Findings

### 1. 🔴 RED — Author waives or assigns film royalties beyond cinema-hall exhibition (CR-09)
**Clause:** C4
> The Author waives all rights to any royalty or share of revenue from satellite, OTT, streaming, music or any other exploitation of the film

Clause 2 makes the lump sum the only payment and waives the author's share of royalties from OTT, satellite and music. The Copyright Act makes such a waiver void for authors of literary works included in a film.

**Law:** The Copyright Act, 1957, s.18; The Copyright Act, 1957, s.19  
**Suggested change:** Delete the waiver; add equal royalty sharing for non-theatrical exploitation with half-yearly statements and audit rights.  
**Confidence:** high

### 2. 🟡 YELLOW — Grant of media "now known or hereafter devised" (CR-08)
**Clause:** C3
> in all media now known or hereafter devised

Clause 1 claims media not yet invented. Such media are not covered unless specifically named, so the clause overreaches and invites dispute.

**Law:** The Copyright Act, 1957, s.18  
**Suggested change:** List the media granted (theatrical, OTT, TV) and keep future media for separate negotiation.  
**Confidence:** high

### 3. 🟡 YELLOW — Waiver of moral rights (credit / integrity) (CR-10)
**Clause:** C6
> The Author waives all moral rights

A blanket waiver of the right to claim authorship and object to distortion; these special rights survive assignment and the extent of waiver is unsettled.

**Law:** The Copyright Act, 1957, s.57  
**Suggested change:** Replace with an agreed adaptation-freedom clause plus a guaranteed credit.  
**Confidence:** medium

### 4. 🟡 YELLOW — Literary work labelled "work for hire" / "contract of service" when the author is not an employee (CR-13)
**Clause:** C5
> shall be a work made for hire and the Producer shall be deemed the first owner of copyright in it

'Work for hire' is not an Indian-law concept for an independent author; the label may be used to deny the author's statutory rights.

**Law:** The Copyright Act, 1957, s.17; The Copyright Act, 1957, s.2(d)  
**Suggested change:** Use an express assignment that complies with s.19 and preserves royalty rights.  
**Confidence:** medium
